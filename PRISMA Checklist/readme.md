# ✅ PRISMA 2020 Checklist

:@ This folder contains the completed **PRISMA 2020 checklist** for the review.

For each of the 27 PRISMA items, the checklist gives the section, table or figure of the paper where the item is reported. Items that point outside the paper refer to:

- the **OSF registration** and its update of 24 November 2026: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/736NS
- the **files in this repository** (extraction records, quality appraisal scores and row-level performance data)

Items that do not apply to this review, such as meta-analysis and certainty of evidence, are marked as not performed.

**Reference:** Page MJ, McKenzie JE, Bossuyt PM, et al. The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting systematic reviews. *BMJ* 2021;372:n71. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71
